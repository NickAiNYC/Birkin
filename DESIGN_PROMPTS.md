## 📹 Recording Setup Guide

For the 60-second demo video:

**Lighting:**
- Key light: Ring light or window light from front-right
- Fill light: Soft reflected light from left
- Background: Neutral (white or dark gray wall)

**Camera Setup:**
- iPhone in landscape mode (for agent interface)
- MacBook showing terminal output (for governance-check.sh)
- Desk setup: minimalist, uncluttered, professional

**Audio:**
- Clear microphone (AirPods Pro or external condenser mic)
- Quiet room (no keyboard clacking, background noise)
- Voiceover: Deliberate pace, emphasis on governance proofs

**Color Grading:**
- Boost greens (governance checkmarks)
- Shadows: navy tones
- Highlights: keep monitor whites clean
